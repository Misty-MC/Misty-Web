## The window

On the left, the sidebar gives access to every page:

| Button | What it opens |
|---|---|
| Play | The home page: account, profile, version and the **Play** button |
| Profiles, mods and modpacks | Your [profiles](/docs/profiles) and their content |
| Texture packs (experimental) | The [texture pack editor](/docs/texture-editor) |
| Release notes | What changed in each version of Misty |
| Open the game folder | The game folder, in the Windows Explorer |
| Settings (at the bottom) | Accounts, language, memory, versions… See [Settings](/docs/settings) |

On the far right of the home page, the vertical **SKIN** tab opens the [skin and capes](/docs/skins) page.

## Start a game

The bar at the bottom of the home page holds everything you need:

1. **Account.** At the first launch, Misty creates an offline account called *Misty*. To play on online servers, sign in with your Microsoft account. See [Accounts](/docs/accounts).
2. **Profile.** *Quick play* uses the shared game folder. Pick one of your [profiles](/docs/profiles) to play with its own mods and worlds.
3. **Version.** Pick the Minecraft version. The list opens on the selected version, so the ones just before and after are easy to reach.
4. **Mod loader.** *Vanilla* plays the game without mods. Misty only lists the loaders available for the chosen version.
5. Click **Play**.

The first time, Misty downloads the game, its libraries, its assets and the right Java version. The progress shows in the Play button. Then the status reads *"… is running, have fun!"*.

> [!NOTE]
> Each version is downloaded only once. The next launches start right away.
