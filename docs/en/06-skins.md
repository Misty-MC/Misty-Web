The **SKIN** tab, on the far right of the home page, changes the skin and the cape of your Microsoft account. The change applies everywhere you play.

> [!NOTE]
> This tab needs a Microsoft account. Without one, it shows a full 3D Steve and offers to sign in. See [Accounts](/docs/accounts).

## Change your skin

1. Click **Choose a skin (PNG)…** and pick your file.
2. Choose the arm model: **Classic (Steve)** or **Slim (Alex)**.
3. Check the preview, then click **Apply skin**.

The skin must be a PNG image of **64×64** or **64×32** pixels.

The preview is a real 3D model: **drag it to rotate** it and see your cape from behind.

## Capes

Every cape on your account is shown with a preview. One click equips a cape; **None** removes it. The change shows immediately and is sent to Mojang right after.

## Fast and offline-friendly

Your skin and capes are loaded in the background when Misty starts and kept on your disk, so the tab opens instantly.

When Mojang limits the number of requests, Misty keeps showing your last known skin and capes, tells you with a message, and checks again by itself once the limit is lifted.
